1. THE MECHANICS OF CANDLESTICK FORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every single candle on this 1-day timeframe chart captures a specific day's market 
action across 4 key data points:
  * Open  (O) -> Starting price when the stock market opens (9:15 AM).
  * Close (C) -> Ending price when the stock market shuts (3:30 PM).
  * High  (H) -> Highest price traded during that session.
  * Low   (L) -> Lowest price traded during that session.

The color indicates who controlled the day:
  * GREEN CANDLE (Bullish) -> Close is HIGHER than Open (Buyers won the day).
  * RED CANDLE   (Bearish) -> Close is LOWER than Open (Sellers won the day).
  * THE WICKS    (Shadows) -> The lines sticking out represent extreme prices
                              tested but ultimately rejected by the market.


2. OVERALL HISTORICAL TREND ANALYSIS (OT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The chart maps out three distinct market cycles spanning over several months:

  * THE DOWNTREND (Left Section):
    Characterized by large, continuous red candles dragging the price down 
    from approximately 130+ INR to below 100 INR. This indicates aggressive 
    institutional supply and broad selling panic.

  * THE CONSOLIDATION PHASE (Middle Section):
    The price stabilizes between 95 INR and 113 INR, carving out a base. 
    Candles become smaller, meaning a state of equilibrium between buyers 
    and sellers where accumulation takes place.

  * THE MOMENTUM RALLY (Right Section):
    The asset showcases massive, explosive green candles leading up to the 
    current price of 118.00 INR. This vertical price movement represents an 
    inflow of high-volume bullish momentum.


3. STRATEGIC TAKEAWAYS AND LEARNINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Support Floors Build Value: 
    The 95-100 INR area acted as a reliable "Support Zone". Every time the price 
    dipped here, buying interest picked up, creating long lower wicks.

  * Resistance Ceilings Halt Rallies: 
    The dashed horizontal line at 122.62 INR is the next major objective. 
    Historically, the price reversed here; it will act as a psychological barrier 
    where supply is expected to increase.

  * Body Size Equals Strength: 
    The recent long-bodied green candles show strong institutional accumulation 
    with zero retracement, suggesting a high probability of challenging the 
    122.62 INR overhead resistance line.
