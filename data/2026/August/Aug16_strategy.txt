DATE: August 16, 2026
TOPIC: 9 vs 21 EMA Trend Following Strategy

========================================================================
CORE CONCEPT: MOMENTUM CROSSOVER
========================================================================
OBJECTIVE: 
Capture the "meat" of a trend by waiting for the fast momentum (9) 
to overtake the slower trend (21).

INDICATORS:
1. 9 EMA (Exponential Moving Average) - Color: Green (Fast)
2. 21 EMA (Exponential Moving Average) - Color: Red (Slow)

========================================================================
THE SIGNALS
========================================================================

[ ] BULLISH ENTRY (LONG)
    1. Wait for the 9 EMA to cross ABOVE the 21 EMA.
    2. CONFIRMATION: The candle causing the cross must close strong.
    3. RIDE: Stay in the trade as long as Price > 9 EMA.

[ ] BEARISH ENTRY (SHORT)
    1. Wait for the 9 EMA to cross BELOW the 21 EMA.
    2. CONFIRMATION: The candle causing the cross must close red.
    3. RIDE: Stay in the trade as long as Price < 9 EMA.

========================================================================
CRITICAL FAILURE CONDITION: "THE CHOP"
========================================================================
WHERE IT FAILS: 
Sideways / Ranging Markets.

IDENTIFICATION:
- The 9 and 21 lines are moving horizontally (flat).
- The lines are constantly crossing each other (Tangled).
- Price is stuck between Support and Resistance.

RULE OF THUMB:
If there is no clear angle (slope) to the lines, DO NOT TOUCH IT.
This strategy requires a TREND to work.

========================================================================
EXIT STRATEGY (TAKE PROFIT)
========================================================================
1. Aggressive: Exit when the candle closes back below the 9 EMA.
2. Conservative: Exit when the 9 EMA crosses back below the 21 EMA.
