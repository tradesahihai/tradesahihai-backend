PUNJAB NATIONAL BANK (PNB) STOCK CHART ANALYSIS & CANDLESTICKS EXPLAINED
======================================================================

1. WHAT IS A CANDLESTICK?
-------------------------
Every individual candle on a chart tells the visual story of a single day
of trading. Each shape looks like a real candle with a thick block (the body)
and thin lines sticking out of the top and bottom (the wicks or shadows). 

Each candle provides exactly four data points from that trading day:
* Open:  The stock price when the market opened at 9:15 AM.
* Close: The stock price when the market closed at 3:30 PM.
* High:  The absolute highest price traded during that day.
* Low:   The absolute lowest price traded during that day.


2. THE COLOR CODE (HOW A CANDLE FORMS)
--------------------------------------
The candle colors reveal who won the daily battle between the Buyers 
(pushing prices up) and the Sellers (dragging prices down).

A. Green Candle (Buyers Won)
   * Core Rule: The stock price closed HIGHER than it opened.
   * Body: The bottom of the solid rectangle is the Open price, 
     and the top of the rectangle is the Close price.
   * Wicks: The top point of the line is the High; the bottom point is the Low.

B. Red Candle (Sellers Won)
   * Core Rule: The stock price closed LOWER than it opened.
   * Body: The top of the solid rectangle is the Open price, 
     and the bottom of the rectangle is the Close price.
   * Wicks: The top point of the line is the High; the bottom point is the Low.


3. CHART ANALYSIS FOR PNB
--------------------------
The chart maps out the multi-month trajectory of Punjab National Bank:

* The Downward Trend (Left to Middle): From February to mid-April, heavy 
  selling pressure is marked by consecutive tall red candles. This drove 
  the price down from roughly 130 INR to below 100 INR.
* The Stabilization (Middle to Right): Around May and June, the price stabilized 
  and consolidated. Long lower wicks in this region highlight active buying interest 
  whenever the stock dipped too low, preventing deeper falls.
* The Bullish Breakout (Far Right): Entering August, massive buying pressure is 
  visible via long green candles lifting the stock sharply up to its current 
  position at 118.00 INR, rapidly moving toward the dashed resistance line at 122.62.
