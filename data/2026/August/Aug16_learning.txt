========================================================================
STRATEGY OVERVIEW: TRADING THE HAMMER
========================================================================
A Hammer is a bullish reversal pattern that forms during a downtrend. 
In stock trading, unlike crypto, we rely heavily on "Volume" and "Gap" 
mechanics to validate the move.

The Psychology: 
1. Sellers push price down (Long Lower Wick).
2. Buyers aggressively reject low prices.
3. Price closes near the open (Small Body).

========================================================================
PHASE 1: THE SETUP (CONTEXT IS KING)
========================================================================
CRITICAL RULE: Do not buy a hammer in a sideways market.

[ ] 1. EXISTING DOWNTREND
    - Ensure the stock has dropped for 3-5 consecutive days.
    - OR the stock has hit a major support level.

[ ] 2. THE HAMMER SHAPE
    - Lower wick must be at least 2x the size of the body.
    - Little to no upper wick.
    - Color: Green is strongest, Red is acceptable if other factors align.

========================================================================
PHASE 2: THE CONFIRMATION (AVOIDING TRAPS)
========================================================================
In stocks, a hammer alone is not a buy signal. You need two confirmations.

[ ] 1. VOLUME SPIKE (The Fuel)
    - Check the volume bar on the day the Hammer forms.
    - It must be HIGHER than the average volume of the previous few days.
    - High Volume + Long Wick = Institutional Buying (Smart Money).

[ ] 2. THE "GREEN LIGHT" CANDLE (The Trigger)
    - Do NOT buy on the Hammer day.
    - Wait for the NEXT trading day.
    - The price must close HIGHER than the Hammer's high.

========================================================================
PHASE 3: EXECUTION GUIDE
========================================================================

ENTRY POINT (BUY):
- Enter the trade the moment price breaks ABOVE the High of the Hammer candle.

STOP LOSS (PROTECTION):
- Place a Hard Stop just BELOW the Lowest Point (Wick) of the Hammer.
- Logic: If price drops below the wick, the reversal failed. Get out.

PROFIT TARGET (EXIT):
- Aim for a 1:2 Risk/Reward Ratio.
- Formula: (Entry Price - Stop Loss) * 2 + Entry Price = Target.

========================================================================
WARNING: THE HANGING MAN TRAP
========================================================================
If you see a "Hammer" shape at the TOP of an uptrend (after weeks of 
green days), it is NOT a Hammer.

- It is called a HANGING MAN.
- It signals a potential crash.
- DO NOT BUY.
